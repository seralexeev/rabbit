# 2026-10-03 power rails from telemetry

Offline, from the Forge exports of 1–3 Oct (power 50 Hz, roboclaw 50 Hz, jetson 1 Hz). Design consequences are in `reports/2026-10-03-architecture-2.0.md` ("Силовая часть"); diagram `reports/media/power-2.0.svg` (generated by `power-2.0.py`).

Hardware as found: NEEWER PS099E on the V-mount plate (99 Wh, 4S, plate/D-Tap 14 A max, 100 W rated, pass-through charging); 2× Pololu 37D 70:1 12 V (stall 5.5 A) on a RoboClaw 2x30A behind a Pololu D36V50F12; AGFRC A50BHL servo on a D36V50F6 via the PCA9685; INA4235 with 10 mΩ shunts (8.19 A range). Whether the Jetson barrel is on its own 12 V buck, the motors' buck or the battery is not known (whiteboard and blog disagree).

| Rail | Median | p99 | Peak |
|---|---|---|---|
| Battery current | 1.26 A | 2.69 A | 7.64 A at 13.55 V (104 W), 7% below the shunt range |
| Battery voltage | 15.8 V | | 14.1 V min; ~0.7 V sag at 7.6 A (~0.1 Ω) |
| RoboClaw supply (12 V buck) | 11.9 V | | 11.3 V at 3.4 + 3.6 A motor current, duty 0.59 (~4.1 A from the buck, its limit) |
| One motor | 0.03 A | 0.45 A | 3.3–4.7 A on starts; single 7–34 A samples are read glitches |
| 6 V servo rail | 0.016 A | 0.04 A | 5.9 A, 5.56 V min; 80% of the > 1 A samples are while the angle changes (servo acceleration, not stalling at full lock) |
| Jetson module (VDD_IN) | 8.5 W | 12.8 W | 15 W |

- The motors' 12 V buck is an accidental current limit (~54 W for both motors). Feeding the RoboClaw straight from the pack (as `plans/pcb-rabbit-board.md` decides) without RoboClaw current limits (3 A/channel) and max duty 0.71 would let two stalled motors draw ~15 A at 16.8 V, above the plate's 14 A: the BMS would cut the computers too.
- If the Jetson shares the motors' buck, a stall that drives the buck into current limit is a candidate for the mid-manoeuvre reboot of 1 Oct (blog #168). No power data covers that reboot.
- The battery shunt needs 2 mΩ (or the readings need a clipped flag).
