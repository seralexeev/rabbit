# 2026-10-03 Body PCB (deck 2)

Offline (the robot was off). A KiCad 10 board for the Rabbit 2.0 body, generated by code, replacing the acrylic deck 2 between the kit's lower plate (motors, servo, Jetson) and upper plate (battery, ZED, lidar mast). Russian report with tables and the mounting list: `reports/2026-10-03-body-pcb.md`. Project: `pcb/rabbit-body/`; `pcb/rabbit-board/` (v1) untouched. No robot or Forge code changed.

## Result

- 4 layers, 1.6 mm, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner, 120.5 × 269.9 mm, outline and standoff holes taken from the top face of the kit plate in `model/chassis.step`.
- Everything on the top side:
  - Pi 4 on M2.5 × 6 mm standoffs, wired by a 40-pin ribbon (J3);
  - RoboClaw 2x30A on M3 × 6 mm standoffs, terminals to the rear;
  - D24V90F5, D36V50F12, D36V50F6;
  - Pololu #2813 switch, 7 ATO fuses (2 × Keystone 3557 each);
  - INA4235 with 2/10/5/10 mΩ shunts on channels 1–4, address 0x41;
  - PCA9685 + 4 servo headers, DS3231 + CR1220;
  - TPS73733 for the ToF rail, 3 × AO3400A;
  - 24 connectors on the edges facing their devices.
- The bottom side has no parts: the Jetson and the motors are under it.
- DRC with JLCPCB-level rules: 0 violations of any severity, 0 unconnected (`fab/drc.json`). A netlist check (`gen/check_netlist.py`) replaces ERC.
- Fab: Gerber + Excellon zip, JLC BOM/CPL for 60 SMD placements (21 lines, all with LCSC numbers), a full BOM with the 30 hand-soldered lines and the modules, the board STEP, and `outline.dxf` with every hole ≥ 2 mm. `kit_plate.dxf` holds the whole kit plate with its 96 holes and slots.
- Estimate: ~US$200–260 for 5 bare + 2 SMD-assembled boards with THT parts and shipping.

## Toolchain

- KiCad 10.0.6: the brew cask needs sudo for its demos, so `KiCad.app` was copied from the cached DMG into `/Applications/KiCad`.
- Python generator on KiCad's bundled Python 3.9 (`pcbnew` SWIG works headless).
- Freerouting 2.4.1 (jar in `data/tools/`, OpenJDK 27 from brew).
- `kicad-cli` for zone fill, DRC, Gerbers, drill, CPL, STEP and 3D renders.
- build123d (the `model/` venv) for the outline and the envelope STEP models of the Pi, RoboClaw, Pololu modules, ATO clips, DSBGA-16 and XT30.
- Rejected:
  - atopile: layout, 3D and fab still need KiCad;
  - SKiDL: `pcbnew` has no Python netlist import;
  - tscircuit: no module models, no current-sized pours.
- The netlist is `gen/design.py` (parts, pins, nets, placement); power routing is `gen/routing.py`. `gen/make.sh` runs everything.

## Decisions and numbers

- **Stack.** Deck 1 top is 24.8 mm above the floor. The Jetson dev kit is 34.77 mm tall (carrier spec fig. 4-2).
  - **Deck 1 → PCB: 45 mm** (today ~40 by the 75 mm wheel scale in the 03.10 photo). At 40 mm the Jetson fan has ~3.5 mm and the 37D motors nearly touch the board.
  - **PCB → deck 3: 45 mm** (as today). The tallest parts are 23–30 mm (Pi USB, RoboClaw, C1, XT60 with its plug); the Pi cooler must stay ≤ 35 mm.
  - Resulting heights: PCB at 69.8 mm, deck 3 at 116.4 mm above the floor.
- **Star ground.** Solid GND on In1. The motor return is its own net `GND_MOT` (B.Cu pour), joined to GND only by NT1 at the XT60 minus.
- **Current paths.** Battery chain pours on F and B, 7.6–9 mm, neck 3.6 mm × 2 layers (~16 A at +20 °C); motor B+ / `GND_MOT` 11 mm. Stitching uses 55 vias of 0.8/0.4.
- **Why 1 oz, not 2 oz.** The 0.4 mm DSBGA needs 0.1 mm features, which JLCPCB does not offer with 2 oz outer copper. Current comes from width on two layers instead.
- **INA4235 ALERT is not connected.** B2 is an inner ball and cannot escape without via-in-pad. GPIO24 has a pull-up to TP8; `ina.py` polls.
- **Deviations from the wiring doc.** All listed in the report §5:
  - Pi 5 V through the ribbon (~65 mV at 2 A) instead of AWG20 wires;
  - on-board ATO fuses instead of the Blue Sea block;
  - an LDO instead of the Pololu 3.3 V module;
  - an optional E-stop loop jumper (J5);
  - the USB hub sits off-board under deck 3, fed by J19.
- **Motor-drive zone.** The motor drive is one contiguous block at the rear left, so a later BLDC revision (e.g. 2× DRV8316 + STM32G4 or STSPIN32G4) can replace it without moving the rest:
  - area X −59.4…+7.2, Y −135…−34.7: 6544 mm², 23% of the 28,661 mm² board; ~7700 mm² with F2 and R3 on its edge;
  - its own feed: F2 10 A → 5 mΩ shunt (INA ch3) → `MOT_BAT`;
  - its own return: `GND_MOT` to the star at the XT60;
  - one signal header (J4) and the encoder connectors on the rear edge.
- **Cable passthroughs.**
  - Edge connectors take the sensors and the motors.
  - Jetson DC, the console and Ethernet go down outside the waist and the right side.
  - The ZED USB cable passes a cut-out (X 23.2…31.0, Y 64.8…78.6) aligned with the kit plates' front-right slot.

## Things that bit

- **The `pcbnew` zone filler ignores hole and board-edge clearances.** Its fills touched NPTH holes and the outline. Fill with `kicad-cli pcb drc --refill-zones --save-board` (`gen/fill.sh`).
- **KiCad's DSN export writes every copper zone as a Specctra plane.** A full-board GND fill on F/B made Freerouting treat the outer layers as taken. The DSN is exported without the GND fills; In1 is retyped `power`.
- **Freerouting's multi-threaded optimiser creates clearance violations** (it says so itself). Use `-mt 1`.
- **Freerouting is deterministic for a given DSN.** Twelve re-runs of the same input left the same net unrouted every time. What fixed the three cases was changing the input:
  - moving the XT30 so it stays inside the outline;
  - moving the CR1220 holder away from ToF FL;
  - 0.15 mm signal tracks instead of 0.2 (three variants run in parallel, the best SES imported).
- **The DSN export ignores inner Edge.Cuts cut-outs.** Freerouting routed a track through the ZED cut-out until a keepout rule area was added over it.
- The verified board is kept in `data/pcb-route/good-final.kicad_pcb`.
- **Signals crossing single-layer pours split them into islands.** DRC flagged MOT_BAT and GND_MOT. Track keepouts over every power pour fixed it; vias stay allowed.
- **KiCad 10's library lacks 3D models for XT30PW and SOT-223-6.** `build_board.py` substitutes them.
- **KiCad has no `PAGE_INFO` in Python.** The A3-portrait paper is patched into the saved file.
- **The JLC parts API** (`jlcpcb.com/api/overseas-pcb-order/v1/shoppingCart/smtGood/selectSmtComponentList`) answers by keyword and C number and gives stock. The INA4235 (C33440856) had 1 piece in stock on 2026-10-03.
